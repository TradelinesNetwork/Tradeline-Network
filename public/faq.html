Getting Started
What is an authorized user tradeline?

An authorized user (AU) tradeline is when a primary cardholder with strong credit history adds you as an authorized user on their credit card. The card's payment history, credit limit, and age are then reported to at least two of the three major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion) on your credit file — typically raising your score.

How long until a tradeline shows on my credit report?

Most tradelines post within one full reporting cycle — typically 15 to 30 days from the cardholder's next statement close date. Every listing shows the exact statement date and reporting window before you buy.

Is this legal?

Yes. Being added as an authorized user on someone else's credit card is 100% legal under U.S. federal law. Credit bureaus have reported AU tradelines for decades. It becomes illegal only if the tradeline is used to commit fraud — for example, misrepresenting your identity on a loan application.

Buying & Payment
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept USDT (TRC-20, ERC-20, BEP-20, Solana), Bitcoin (BTC, BEP-20, RENEC), and Litecoin (LTC). Crypto payments are irreversible and fast, which is why we can guarantee posting and offer better pricing than card-based competitors.

How do I pay?

After you add a tradeline to your cart and enter authorized user information, we display a merchant wallet address for your selected crypto and network. You send the payment from your own wallet or exchange, then paste the transaction hash on the checkout page. Our system verifies the payment on-chain, and your order enters the fulfillment queue.

What if I send the wrong amount?

Underpayments are automatically flagged. We'll contact you to send the difference, or we can refund the amount received (minus network fees) if you'd rather cancel.

Is my payment refundable?

Orders are fully refundable up until your AU information is submitted to the cardholder. Once submitted, refunds are governed by our Refund Policy and the posting guarantee.

Guarantees & Reporting
What is your posting guarantee?

Every tradeline we sell is guaranteed to post to at least two of the three major bureaus within the stated reporting window. If it fails to post to two bureaus, you receive a full credit toward another tradeline — no questions asked.

How long does the tradeline stay on my report?

Each listing shows the reporting duration — usually two full statement cycles (~60 days). After removal, the tradeline may remain visible on your report as historical data for up to two additional years, depending on the bureau.

Will my credit score definitely go up?

Most customers see a meaningful score increase, but results depend on your overall credit profile — number of open accounts, derogatory marks, utilization, etc. We cannot guarantee a specific point increase and do not claim to be a credit repair service.

Security & Privacy
Is my personal information safe?

Yes. Authorized user details (name, DOB, last-4 SSN, address) are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they touch our database. Only the assigned processor for your order can decrypt them, and records are deleted 90 days after the reporting cycle closes.

Do you share my info with the credit bureaus?

No — we do not communicate with the bureaus directly. Your AU information is passed only to the cardholder who adds you to their account. The cardholder's bank then reports the tradeline to the bureaus as part of their normal reporting.

Do you serve customers in Georgia?

No. Due to state-specific credit repair regulations, we do not sell tradelines to residents of Georgia. All other U.S. states are supported.
