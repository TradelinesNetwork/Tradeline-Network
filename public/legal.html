Privacy Policy

Tradelines Network LLC ("Tradelines Network," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you create an account or place an order
  • Authorized user details: name, date of birth, last four digits of Social Security Number, and mailing address of the person being added as an authorized user — collected solely for AU processing
  • Payment information: cryptocurrency transaction hashes and wallet addresses, used solely to verify and confirm payment on-chain
  • Communications: emails, form submissions, and support tickets you send us

Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and referring URLs
  • Pages visited, time spent on site, and click patterns via analytics tools
  • Cookies and local storage used to maintain session state, cart data, and preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your tradeline orders
  • To communicate with you about your orders, account, and support requests
  • To send marketing communications if you have opted in (you may opt out at any time)
  • To comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud, and enforce our terms
  • To improve our platform through aggregated, anonymized analytics

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. We share your information only as follows:

  • Card holders: Authorized user information (name, DOB, SSN last 4, address) is shared with the card holder who is adding you to their account, as required to process the AU addition
  • Blockchain verification: Transaction hashes are used to verify payments on public blockchain networks. No personal financial details are shared with third parties.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or court order

4. Data Security

We use 256-bit AES encryption for all stored personal data and TLS 1.3 for all data in transit. Identity verification documents are stored in an encrypted, access-controlled vault and are accessible only to staff directly involved in processing your order.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and order information for 7 years to comply with financial record-keeping requirements. Identity verification documents are deleted within 90 days of your order's reporting period closing, unless we are legally required to retain them longer.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To submit a data request, email marketplacetradeline@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and cart. We use analytics cookies to understand site usage. You may disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions: marketplacetradeline@gmail.com or write to Tradelines Network LLC, 1800 Vine St Suite 220, Los Angeles, CA 90028.

Terms & Conditions

By accessing or using the Tradelines Network platform ("Service"), you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. Please read them carefully before placing any order.

1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and a legal resident of the United States (excluding Georgia) to purchase tradelines through Tradelines Network. By placing an order, you represent that you meet these requirements.

2. Nature of Service

Tradelines Network facilitates the placement of customers as authorized users on third-party credit card accounts. We are not a credit repair organization as defined under the Credit Repair Organizations Act (CROA). We do not guarantee any specific improvement in your credit score. Results vary based on your individual credit profile.

3. Purchase Process

  • All orders must be placed before the stated Purchase Deadline for the selected tradeline
  • Payment must clear before the AU information is submitted to the card holder
  • A valid cryptocurrency wallet and transaction hash are required to confirm payment for all orders
  • Orders are non-cancellable once AU information has been submitted to the card holder

4. Posting Guarantee

Tradelines Network guarantees that each purchased tradeline will be reported to at least two (2) of the three major credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion) within the stated reporting period. If a tradeline fails to post to at least two bureaus, Tradelines Network will issue a full credit to the customer's account. This credit must be used within 12 months.

The posting guarantee does not apply if: (a) the customer provided incorrect authorized user information; (b) the customer's credit file was frozen at the relevant bureaus during the reporting period; (c) the card issuer removed the AU due to fraud concerns related to the customer.

5. Geographic Restrictions

Tradelines Network does not sell tradelines to customers whose billing address or authorized user address is located in the state of Georgia. All other U.S. states are served.

6. Payment Terms

Tradelines Network accepts cryptocurrency payments only — USDT, Bitcoin (BTC), and Litecoin (LTC). Credit card payments are not accepted. Every order must be confirmed with a valid on-chain transaction hash. All prices are in U.S. dollars. Tradelines Network reserves the right to change pricing at any time without notice; however, the price shown at checkout is locked for your order.

7. Prohibited Uses

  • Purchasing tradelines with intent to commit bank fraud or materially misrepresent your creditworthiness on a loan application
  • Reselling Tradelines Network tradelines without an active Broker Agreement

  • Attempting to circumvent our fraud detection systems

8. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Tradelines Network's total liability for any claim arising from your use of the Service shall not exceed the amount you paid for the specific order giving rise to the claim. Tradelines Network is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

Refund Policy

We want every customer to get value from every order. This policy describes exactly when a refund or credit is available.

1. Before AU Submission — Full Refund

If you request cancellation before your authorized user information has been submitted to the cardholder, you receive a full refund in the same cryptocurrency you paid with, minus the network transaction fee. Requests must be sent to marketplacetradeline@gmail.com with your order ID.

2. After AU Submission — Governed by Posting Guarantee

Once AU information is submitted to the cardholder, the order is non-cancellable. It is instead covered by our Posting Guarantee: if the tradeline fails to post to at least two of the three major bureaus within the stated reporting window, you receive a full credit toward another tradeline of equal or lesser value. Credits must be redeemed within 12 months.

3. When Refunds Are Not Available

  • Customer provided incorrect authorized user information (name, DOB, address, last-4 SSN).
  • Customer's credit file was frozen at the relevant bureaus during the reporting period.
  • The cardholder's bank removed the AU due to fraud concerns tied to the customer.
  • Order was placed by a resident of a restricted state (currently Georgia).
  • Chargebacks or crypto payment reversals initiated for orders fulfilled as described. See our Fraud Policy.

4. Platform Fees

Broker monthly subscription fees and the Credit Partner onboarding fee are non-refundable once the applicable service window has begun. Broker subscriptions can be cancelled at any time to prevent future charges.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email marketplacetradeline@gmail.com from the address on the order, include your order ID, and state the reason. We respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are sent within 3 business days.

KYC / AML Disclosure

Tradelines Network accepts cryptocurrency payments and connects buyers with credit-adjacent services. To protect our platform, our customers, and our credit partners, we operate a Know-Your-Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) program aligned with U.S. FinCEN guidance for financial-services facilitators.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Full legal name, date of birth, and residential address of the authorized user.
  • Last four digits of the Social Security Number of the authorized user.
  • Government-issued photo ID for orders above $1,000 in a rolling 30-day window, or when triggered by our risk engine.
  • The originating cryptocurrency wallet address, the transaction hash, the amount, and the timestamp for every payment.

2. How We Use It

KYC data is used solely to (a) confirm the identity of the person the tradeline will be reported on, (b) meet lawful reporting obligations, and (c) screen for prohibited persons and jurisdictions. It is not sold, rented, or shared for marketing. Cardholders receive only the AU information required to add the user to their card.

3. Sanctions and Prohibited Persons

We screen every order against the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list and comparable sanctions lists. Orders that match are cancelled, funds are held pending guidance, and the incident is reported where required by law.

4. Suspicious Activity

We monitor for structuring, unusual velocity, mismatched identity documents, and repeat use of the same AU across unrelated accounts. Suspicious orders are held for manual review and may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

5. Data Retention

AU personal information is encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest and deleted 90 days after the reporting cycle closes. KYC records for orders that trigger enhanced review are retained for 5 years as required by U.S. record-keeping rules.

6. Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or request deletion where legally permitted, by emailing marketplacetradeline@gmail.com. Deletion requests that conflict with mandatory retention obligations are honored to the extent the law allows.

Fraud Policy

Tradelines Network takes fraud prevention seriously — both to protect our customers and to maintain the integrity of our platform. This policy describes how we detect, respond to, and penalize fraudulent activity.

1. Zero Tolerance for Fraud

Tradelines Network operates a zero-tolerance fraud policy. Any customer, card holder, or broker found engaging in fraudulent activity will be permanently banned from the platform, forfeit all active orders and credits, and may be reported to relevant law enforcement authorities.

2. What Constitutes Fraud on This Platform

By Buyers

  • Providing false authorized user information (fake names, SSNs, or addresses)
  • Submitting fraudulent identity documents
  • Initiating cryptocurrency payment reversals or disputes for orders that were fulfilled as described
  • Using purchased tradelines to commit loan fraud or materially misrepresent creditworthiness on credit applications
  • Creating multiple accounts to circumvent bans or pricing tiers

By Card Holders (Credit Partners)

  • Listing cards that do not meet stated quality requirements
  • Accepting payment and failing to add the authorized user
  • Removing authorized users before the end of the stated reporting period without notifying Tradelines Network
  • Operating cards with concealed derogatory history

By Brokers

  • Reselling at pricing that violates a signed Broker Agreement
  • Submitting orders on behalf of clients in restricted states
  • Misrepresenting Tradelines Network's services or guarantees to end clients

3. Fraud Detection

Tradelines Network employs automated and manual fraud detection, including: blockchain transaction verification, IP and device fingerprinting, order pattern analysis, and third-party fraud scoring. Suspicious orders may be held for manual review before processing.

4. Reporting Fraud

If you believe you have been the victim of fraud involving Tradelines Network — whether by another customer, a card holder, or a broker — contact us immediately at marketplacetradeline@gmail.com. Include as much detail as possible. We investigate all reports within 2 business days.

5. Chargebacks

Initiating a chargeback for a fulfilled order is considered fraud. Tradelines Network contests all fraudulent chargebacks and provides documentation of order fulfillment to payment processors. Customers who initiate unjustified chargebacks are permanently banned and may be pursued for the original payment plus dispute fees.

6. Contact

To report fraud: marketplacetradeline@gmail.com
